
## Liquid Fill Gauge — wave surface reference/adaptation

The sine-wave surface in app/liquid-surface.ts adapts the animated wave approach
of Liquid Fill Gauge by Curtis Bratton to a descending, widening SVG surface.
Source: https://gist.github.com/brattonc/5e5ce9beee483220e2f6
No D3 runtime or gauge UI is bundled.
